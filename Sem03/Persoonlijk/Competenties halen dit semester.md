
## Management
Dit is mijn hoofdprofiel. Dit is must have dat ik dit semester moet kunnen aantonen.
### Analyse 
Analyzing multiple operational and tactical organizational processes, including the quality of associated data and current and future ICT provision

Analyzing the coherence of bottlenecks and cause-effect relationships

Determining ICT requirements based on the needs of relevant stakeholders

### Advise 
Advising coherent solutions for bottlenecks in organizational structure, process structure, and information provision

Advising on new ICT options, including package selection and advice

### Design 
Designing coherent organizational processes: functional organizational structure, process management, and information provision, taking into account security and privacy legislation

Designing interfaces for an application in the application landscape (mappings)

### Realize 
Realizing the introduction and acceptance of procedures in conjunction with new or modified information provision and governance

Generating and validating a proof of concept of an application

Setting up a standard application

### Manage&Control 
Setting up, maintaining, and updating (functional) management processes

Measuring and monitoring organizational processes using data

Identifying the change need of multiple operational and tactical organizational processes

## Embedded Devices
Dit is mijn subdoel/ leuke als het lukt, als ik nog tijd over heb wil ik hieraan werken. 
### Analyse 
Performing a requirements analysis for a (sub)system, including hardware and software, taking into account domain aspects and relevant quality attributes including security, safety, and sustainability

Performing a protocol analysis

Drafting an acceptance test for a system based on quality requirements

### Advise 
Providing technical advice on the architecture of a (part of a) system including hardware and software components, based on the requirements analysis

Advising on linking hardware systems and software components

### Design 
Methodically designing a (part of a) system based on requirements with self-selected hardware and software components

Integrating a hardware interface into an application (e.g. application driver software)

### Realize 
Realizing a designed (part of a) system and using software to realize connections with hardware components

Writing and testing designed (parts of) systems and hardware interfaces (e.g. application driver software)

### Manage&Control 
Assessing a given development environment based on quality requirements

Monitoring, reporting, and assessing a given test development environment
